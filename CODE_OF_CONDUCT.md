# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects and promotes the
dignity, rights, and contributions of all individuals, regardless of
characteristics including race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical
characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or
expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or
social origin, socio-economic position, level of education, or other status.
The same privileges of participation are extended to everyone who participates
in good faith and in accordance with this Covenant.

## Encouraged Behaviors

We agree to act in ways that center our shared values, including:

1. Respecting the purpose of our community and its collaborative practices.
2. Engaging kindly and honestly with others.
3. Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
4. Taking responsibility for our actions and contributions.
5. Gracefully giving and accepting constructive feedback.
6. Committing to repairing harm when it occurs.

## Restricted Behaviors

The following behaviors are violations of this code of conduct:

1. Harassment, including violating clearly expressed boundaries or continuing
   unwanted personal attention after a request to stop.
2. Insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments directed at a person or group.
3. Stereotyping or discrimination based on identity or traits.
4. Inappropriately sexualized behavior in community spaces.
5. Sharing another person's private information without permission.
6. Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm.
7. Impersonation, deliberate misrepresentation, or other conduct that threatens
   the well-being of the community.

## Reporting an Issue

To report a possible violation, contact a repository maintainer through the
repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue when it would
disclose private or sensitive information. Include only the context needed for
the maintainer to understand and respond to the report.

Maintainers will treat reports seriously, investigate them as appropriate, and
seek to protect the safety and confidentiality of everyone involved.

## Enforcement

Depending on the severity and pattern of behavior, maintainers may issue a
warning, limit participation temporarily, suspend participation, or remove a
person from community spaces. Maintainers may skip lower steps when necessary
to protect community safety.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project community spaces and when someone
is publicly representing the project.
